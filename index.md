# Koios Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Koios is an ebook and comic reader for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is built so that your reading stays yours.

## What Koios collects

**Nothing.** Koios does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in.
- No ads.
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- No network connections: Koios does not send anything off your device.

## Your files and reading data

The books and comics you import, your library, reading progress and settings are stored only on your device, inside Koios's own storage. If you use iCloud device backups, iOS may include this data in your backup, under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting Koios removes its data from your device.

## Children

Koios does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If Koios ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at [github.com/BleuBirddy/koios-privacy/issues](https://github.com/BleuBirddy/koios-privacy/issues).
